        How it works

        Three steps

        1. 1

          Search for your stop

          Type any part of its name, like "kuru" or "bus", and pick it from the list.

        2. 2

          See the buses on their way

          Only buses that are still coming to your stop are shown, nearest first.

        3. 3

          Watch the minutes drop

          The page refreshes by itself every few seconds, so there is nothing to press.
